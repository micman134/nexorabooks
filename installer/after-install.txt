Nexora Books is installed.


STARTING IT
-----------
Open it from the Start menu, or from the desktop shortcut if you asked for one.
A window opens with your books in it, and a small console window stays behind it
showing the address your staff use. Leave that console open while anyone is
working — closing it signs everyone out.


SIGNING IN THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
    username   admin
    password   admin123

It will make you choose a new password straight away. Do it, and write it down
somewhere that is not this computer.


YOUR STAFF
----------
They install nothing. On this computer open Settings > Access from other
computers; it shows an address like

    http://192.168.1.20:8756

They open that in Chrome, Edge or Firefox and bookmark it. Give each person
their own login under Settings > Users — never share one account, because an
audit trail is only worth having when it names a person.


THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES
----------------------
1. Settings > Company details — your country, your currency, your address.
   Choose the currency carefully: it is fixed once you post your first
   transaction.
2. Settings > Backup and restore — set the automatic backup, and point the
   second copy at a flash drive or a synced folder. This is the setting that
   matters most on this whole list.
3. Settings > Email — so invoices can go straight to customers as a PDF.
4. Bring in your data — customers, suppliers and opening balances out of a
   spreadsheet, rather than typing them.
5. If you are outside Nigeria: Payroll > Payroll scheme, then Payroll > Check
   the scheme, before you pay anybody.


YOUR BOOKS
----------
Everything you enter is kept on this computer, in one folder, with one file per
company. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing needs the internet. Settings >
Diagnostics will tell you exactly where the folder is.

Uninstalling Nexora Books does not delete your books.
