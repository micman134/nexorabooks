Nexora Books 2.8.0 — how to install it on your Windows computer
=============================================================

You need Python on the computer once, to build the application. After that the
application runs on its own and Python is never needed again on any machine you
copy it to.


STEP 1 — Install Python (once, on the main computer)
----------------------------------------------------
Go to  https://www.python.org/downloads/  and download Python for Windows.

When the installer opens, TICK THE BOX at the bottom that says
"Add python.exe to PATH" before you click Install. This matters.


STEP 2 — Build Nexora Books
-------------------------
Unzip this folder somewhere sensible, for example  C:\Nexora Books

Double-click  build_windows.bat

It installs what it needs, runs all 1,294 tests, and packages everything.
Give it a few minutes the first time. When it finishes you will have:

    dist\NexoraBooks\NexoraBooks.exe          the application
    dist\NexoraBooks-2.8.0-Setup.exe          an installer, if you have Inno Setup

Inno Setup is free, from https://jrsoftware.org/isdl.php. With it installed the
build makes a proper Windows installer that puts Nexora Books in the Start menu,
offers a desktop shortcut, opens the firewall for your office network, and
uninstalls cleanly — without ever touching your books.

Without it you still get a working application: copy the whole
dist\NexoraBooks folder to any Windows computer and run it there.


STEP 3 — Run it
---------------
Run the installer, or double-click  dist\NexoraBooks\NexoraBooks.exe

Windows will ask whether to allow it on the network. Choose PRIVATE NETWORKS
and allow — without this your staff cannot reach it from their computers.

Sign in as       admin
with password    admin123

You will be asked to choose a new password straight away. Do it.


IN A HURRY? TRY IT FIRST WITHOUT BUILDING
------------------------------------------
After Step 1, open a Command Prompt in this folder and run:

    pip install -r requirements.txt
    python seed_demo.py
    Start-NexoraBooks.bat

That loads a demo company with five months of trading, payroll, stock, assets
and reports already in it, so you can look around before committing your own
figures. Sign in as admin / admin123.

When you are ready for your own books, delete the demo folder it created
(the application tells you where) and start fresh.


FIRST THING: YOUR COUNTRY AND YOUR CURRENCY
-------------------------------------------
On the setup screen, pick your country. That fills in your currency, what your
country calls its sales tax, what a tax ID and a registration number are called,
and how dates are written. Change any of it afterwards — you know your country
better than the list does.

Choose the currency carefully. Once you post your first transaction it is fixed,
because every figure is stored as a whole number of its smallest unit and
switching later would relabel your accounts rather than convert them. To keep
books in a second currency, make a second company.


IF YOU ARE NOT IN NIGERIA: SET UP PAYROLL PROPERLY
--------------------------------------------------
Nigeria's tax bands and contributions are built in. Everywhere else, go to
Payroll > Payroll scheme and enter your own bands and contributions.

Then — and this matters — go to Payroll > Check the scheme, take one payslip
whose answer you already know, and make sure the software reproduces it before
you pay anybody from it. Rates you type in yourself are only as right as the
typing, and a wrong payslip is somebody's rent.


YOUR 30 DAYS, AND YOUR LICENCE
------------------------------
A fresh installation runs for 30 days with nothing to enter. After that,
Settings > Licence shows a code for this computer; send it to whoever sold you
the software and paste back the licence they send you.

If the licence lapses, nothing is taken away from you. Every screen still opens,
every report still prints and exports, and you can still take a full backup.
What pauses is entering new transactions.


BEFORE STAFF CAN RAISE REQUISITIONS
-----------------------------------
Under Settings > Users, for each person set:

  * their manager        — where their requisitions go for approval
  * their bank details   — where finance sends the money

Tick "can release the money" on your finance manager, and "signs off
requisitions over the limit" on yourself or a director. Set the limit itself
under Settings > Company details.

Somebody with no manager cannot raise a requisition at all — the system refuses
it rather than letting it sit with nobody to approve it.


YOUR STAFF
----------
They do not install anything. On the main computer open
Settings > Access from other computers. It shows an address like

    http://192.168.1.20:8756

Your staff open that address in Chrome, Edge or Firefox and bookmark it.
Give each person their own login under Settings > Users — never share one
account, because the audit trail is only worth having when it names a person.

The main computer has to be switched on and running Nexora Books for them to work.


WHERE YOUR BOOKS ARE KEPT
-------------------------
Everything lives in one folder on your computer, with one database file per
company. Settings > Backup & restore makes a copy you can put on a flash drive
or in Google Drive. Do this weekly. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.


WHAT YOUR INVOICE EMAILS SAY
----------------------------
If your business says the same thing on every invoice — thank you for
ordering, here is how to pay, send us the receipt — write it once under
Settings > Email, in "What your invoices say". It becomes the starting point
for every invoice, quotation and credit note you email, and can still be edited
on the send screen before anything goes out.

Put {customer}, {first_name}, {number}, {amount}, {due_date}, {item} and the
rest into the wording and the real figures take their place. {item} is what the
first line of the document says, which for a training business is the course
name. The full list, with an example of how yours will read, is on that screen.

A line that mentions something a particular document does not have — a credit
note has no due date — is left out of that message rather than sent with a gap
in it.


MOVING TO A NEW VERSION
-----------------------
Your books are NOT in the program folder. They live in your own Windows
profile, under AppData, and every version opens the same ones. So updating is:
unzip the new version wherever you like, and start it.

One thing catches people out. CLOSE THE OLD VERSION FIRST. Only one copy can
use the address at a time, so if the old one is still running the new one
cannot take over — and until version 2.8.3 it would quietly show you the old
copy instead, which looks exactly like the update having done nothing.

To be sure it is closed: close every Nexora Books window, including any black
command window behind them; then press Ctrl+Shift+Esc and, under Processes, end
anything called NexoraBooks, python.exe or py.exe. Restarting the computer does
the same job with less hunting.

Then start the new version and look under your company name in the top left
corner. It shows the version that is actually running. If it does not say the
one you just unzipped, an older copy is still up.

From 2.8.3 onwards the program checks this for you and says so plainly instead
of opening the older copy.


WHEN YOU INVITE STAFF BY EMAIL
------------------------------
The invitation carries a link, and that link has to point at an address that
works on THEIR computer. If you are sitting at the computer Nexora Books runs
on, your own browser probably says 127.0.0.1 — and 127.0.0.1 means "the
computer I am using", so on your colleague's machine it points at their own
computer and the page will not open.

Nexora Books works this out for you: it finds this computer's address on the
office network and builds the link from that instead. If it cannot find one, it
refuses to send rather than posting a link that cannot work, and tells you so.

If your staff reach these books at some other address — a fixed IP, a name on
the network, anything sitting in front of Nexora Books — put it in under
Settings > Access from other computers, in "The address used in emails". Every
link sent out is built from it.

If somebody has already been sent a link that will not open: set the address,
then invite them again from Settings > Users. The new link replaces the old one.


LOCKED OUT BY TWO-FACTOR SIGN-IN
--------------------------------
If somebody loses the phone that produces their six-digit codes, they use one
of the recovery codes they saved when they set it up. If those are gone too,
any administrator clears it for them from Settings > Users.

And if the person locked out IS the only administrator, so there is nobody left
to ask: close Nexora Books, then double-click

    Reset-TwoFactor

in this folder. (If you are running the built application rather than the
source, open a Command Prompt in the folder holding NexoraBooks.exe and run
"NexoraBooks.exe --reset-two-factor" instead.) It asks which company and which
person, turns the second step off for them, and writes what it did to that
company's audit trail. Passwords, invoices and everything else are untouched.

One thing worth knowing before you turn two-factor on: the six digits depend on
this computer's clock being right. If Windows has drifted by more than half a
minute the codes will be refused, and Nexora Books will tell you so in as many
words rather than leaving you guessing. Settings > Time & language > Date &
time, and turn on "Set time automatically".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
README.md has a troubleshooting section covering the usual problems — the
firewall, the port, a forgotten password, and restoring from a backup.
