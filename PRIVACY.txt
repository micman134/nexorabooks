NEXORA BOOKS — WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR INFORMATION
===============================================


THE SHORT VERSION

Nexora Books runs on your own computer and keeps your books in a file on that
computer. It does not send your accounting data anywhere. There is no cloud
account, no server of ours holding your figures, and no internet connection
required to use it.


WHAT THE SOFTWARE KEEPS, AND WHERE

Everything you type in — your customers, suppliers, staff, invoices, payments,
payroll and ledger — is stored in one folder on your computer, with one
database file for each company. Settings > Diagnostics shows you exactly where.

Attachments you upload, your logo, and your backups are in that same folder.

Nobody else can read it unless they can get at that computer, or at a backup
you have put somewhere else. Protecting the computer, and choosing where your
backups go, is yours to do.


WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER

Three things, all of which you start yourself:

  1. Email you send. If you set up Settings > Email, invoices and statements
     go out through your own mail provider, using your own account. The
     message and its PDF go from you to your customer. We are not in the
     middle of it and we never see it.

  2. Backups you place elsewhere. If you tell the automatic backup to put a
     copy in a folder that syncs to Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, then a
     copy of your books goes there, under that company's terms rather than
     ours. That is usually a very good idea; it is also worth knowing.

  3. A diagnostic report, if you send one. Settings > Diagnostics builds a
     text file describing the installation — version, operating system, how big
     the files are, whether backups are running, and recent errors. It does not
     copy your invoices or your customer list. It is shown to you in full
     before you send it, and it goes nowhere unless you attach it to an email
     yourself.

Two-factor sign-in is worth mentioning here because people assume it must
involve an account somewhere. It does not. The key is generated on your
computer, the QR code that carries it to your phone is drawn on your computer,
and the six digits are worked out on both ends independently. Your phone and
this software never speak to each other, and neither of them speaks to us.

Nothing else. The software does not check for updates, does not report usage,
and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.


YOUR LICENCE

When you buy a licence you give us a machine code from the Licence screen. It
is a one-way fingerprint of the installation: it contains no name, no serial
number and nothing that identifies you or your computer, and it cannot be
turned back into any of those things.

We hold the name you gave us, the machine code, what you paid and when, so that
we can support you and re-issue your key if your computer is replaced. We keep
that while your licence is live and for six years afterwards, so that we can
meet Nigerian tax and record-keeping requirements, and then we delete it.

The licence check happens entirely on your computer. Nexora Books never
contacts us to verify anything, so we cannot tell whether or when you are using
it.


PERSONAL INFORMATION IN YOUR BOOKS

Your books will contain personal information about other people — the names,
addresses, bank details and pay of your customers, suppliers and staff.

That information is yours to look after, not ours. Under the Nigeria Data
Protection Act 2023 you are the one responsible for it. In practical terms:

  * keep the computer locked, and give each person their own login;
  * think about who can reach the folder your backups go into;
  * remember that a backup on a flash drive is a copy of everyone's pay.

The software helps where it can: every user has their own account, the audit
trail records who did what, and payslips are only visible to people whose role
allows it.


CHILDREN

This is accounting software for businesses. It is not intended for use by
children and we do not knowingly hold any information about them.
