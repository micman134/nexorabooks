Electronic invoicing

Read this before you switch anything on.

Nexora Books can build the document the Nigeria Revenue Service asks for, check it, and keep a queue of what has not got through. What it cannot yet do is file on your behalf, because access to the Service's platform comes with credentials and a sandbox that this software has not been tested against.

So rehearsal is the mode that works today. It tells you exactly what would be refused, using your own invoices, and marks everything it produces as a rehearsal. Do not present it to anybody as having filed. What live filing needs.
