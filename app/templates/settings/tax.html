  • Withholding tax is calculated on the amount before VAT — never on the VAT itself.
  • A small company (turnover ₦50 million or less) holding a valid Tax ID does not suffer WHT on transactions of ₦2,000,000 or less. Mark the contact as a small company to apply this.
