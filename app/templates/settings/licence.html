What a lapsed licence does and does not do

Your books are always yours. With no licence you can still open every screen, print and export every report, look at every past transaction, and take a full backup you can restore anywhere. Nothing is deleted, hidden or held back.

What stops is writing new entries — invoices, bills, payments, payroll and journals — until a licence is entered. Nobody is ever signed out over a licence, and no account is ever switched off for you.
