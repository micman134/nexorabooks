How PAYE is worked out here

Each period's pay is annualised, the reliefs are taken off, the result is taxed in bands, and the answer is divided back down. Reliefs are the employee's {{ settings.pension_employee }}% pension, NHF, NHIS, life assurance where recorded, and rent relief — the lower of {{ settings.rent_relief_rate }}% of the rent they actually pay or {{ settings.rent_relief_cap|money }}. Anyone earning no more than the national minimum wage ({{ settings.minimum_wage|money }} a month) pays no PAYE at all. Every payslip shows the working.

The Consolidated Relief Allowance and the old 1% minimum tax no longer exist — they were removed by the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. All rates are editable under {% if can(user, 'admin') %}Payroll settings{% else %}Payroll settings{% endif %}.
