How to read this

The left column is what actually happened — the same figures as your profit and loss for those dates. The right column is those figures with your changes applied, nothing more. There is no model and no forecast: it is arithmetic you could do by hand, done quickly.

The link between price and volume is your assumption, not a finding. The software does not know how many customers you would lose by charging more. What it can tell you is the break-even — how much volume you could afford to lose before the increase stopped being worth having — and that figure is arithmetic.
