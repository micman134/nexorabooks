How this works

Separate everything Each company has its own chart of accounts, customers, suppliers, stock, staff, invoice numbering and user accounts. A mistake in one can never reach another.
Signing in Because user accounts belong to a company's books, opening a different company asks you to sign in again. That is deliberate — it stops someone seeing books they were never given access to.
Backups Backup and restore under Settings always act on the company you have open. Take one for each company.
