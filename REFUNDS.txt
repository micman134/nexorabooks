NEXORA BOOKS — REFUNDS AND SUPPORT
==================================


THE THIRTY DAYS BEFORE YOU BUY

Nexora Books runs in full for 30 days without a licence key. Everything works:
every screen, every report, your real figures, your real staff.

Use it. That is what the trial is for, and it is the reason the refund policy
below can be short — nobody should be buying this without having already run
their own books through it.


IF YOU CHANGE YOUR MIND

Ask within 14 days of buying and we will refund you in full, no questions
asked. Write to support@tavonetworks.tech with the name you bought under.

We will refund the way you paid, within 7 working days of agreeing.


IF IT DOES NOT DO WHAT WE SAID

For 90 days after you buy, if the software does not do what our description
says it does, tell us. We will either fix it or refund you in full — your
choice, not ours.

Please tell us what happened first. Settings > Diagnostics makes a file with
everything we need in it. Most of what gets reported turns out to be something
we can fix in a day.


WHAT WE DO NOT REFUND

  * A licence bought more than 90 days ago that works as described.
  * A licence you can no longer use because your computer was replaced. Tell
    us and we will issue a new key for the new computer, free.
  * Time you spent, or business you lost. See clause 8 of the licence
    agreement.


IF YOUR COMPUTER DIES

Your licence is tied to one computer. If that computer is lost, stolen, broken
or replaced, write to support@tavonetworks.tech with the machine code from the
new one and we will issue a new key. There is no charge and no limit on how
often.

Restore your books from a backup and carry on. This is why the automatic
backup asks you for a second folder — put it on a flash drive or a synced
folder and a dead computer costs you a morning rather than a year.


WHAT SUPPORT INCLUDES

  * Help getting installed and set up.
  * Help bringing your existing books in.
  * Fixing anything that is genuinely broken.
  * A new licence key when your computer changes.

Reach us at support@tavonetworks.tech or +234 808 334 2954, Monday to Friday,
9am to 5pm West Africa Time. We answer within one working day.


WHAT SUPPORT IS NOT

We are not your accountant and we cannot be. We will not tell you what to file,
what rate applies to you, or how to treat a transaction — not because we are
being unhelpful, but because getting that wrong would cost you far more than
this software costs, and an accountant who knows your business is the right
person to ask.

What we will always do is show you how to get the software to do what your
accountant tells you it should.
