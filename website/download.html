Installing it

  1. Download the file and open it

    It lands in your Downloads folder. Double-click it.

  2. Windows may ask whether you are sure

    Choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows says this about any program it has not seen many times before.

  3. Allow it on your private network

    A firewall box appears. Tick Private networks and allow it — without that, nobody else in your office can reach the books.

  4. Sign in and change the password

    Username admin, password admin123. It makes you choose a new one straight away.

  5. Set your country and currency

    Two minutes, on the first screen. Choose the currency carefully — it is fixed once you post your first transaction.

Nothing else to install. No database to set up, no account to create, no internet connection needed after the download.

Your staff do not install anything

Nexora Books runs on one computer and serves everybody else. Your staff open a web address in Chrome, Edge or Firefox and sign in with their own username. Give each person their own account — a shared login makes the audit trail worthless.

In the same office

They use an address like 192.168.1.20:8756, shown on the Access screen. Bookmark it once and it is done.

In another state or country

Join their computers to yours with a free private-network tool such as Tailscale. Your books never leave your machine. The Access screen walks you through it.

Questions people ask first

Where are my books kept?
On your computer, in your own Windows profile. Not on our servers — we do not have a copy and could not produce one if asked.
What happens after 30 days?
You buy a licence for the number of people who sign in. If you do not, every screen still opens, every report still prints and exports, and you can take a full backup. What pauses is entering new transactions.
Does it need the internet?
Only to email an invoice. Everything else works with the connection unplugged.
What if my computer dies?
Restore your backup on the new machine and tell us the new machine code — a replacement licence is free. Set the automatic backup to a flash drive or a synced folder on your first day.
Can my accountant see it?
Give them their own sign-in, or send them the exports. Everything comes out as PDF or spreadsheet.
What does it cost?
From ₦10,000 per person a month, billed a year at a time, and cheaper the more of you there are — ₦9,000 each from five people, ₦8,000 from ten. The exact figure for your size is shown inside the software under Settings › Licence, before you pay anything.
